WASD

World Awareness & State Display

A free, community-driven live game companion that understands your playthrough and gives you as much or as little guidance as you want.

What it is

A companion that reacts to your actual playthrough.

WASD runs alongside supported games and uses live game-state information to provide contextual guidance, progression tracking, route awareness, build information and session analysis.

Current support

Dark Souls III

First supported game

Dark Souls III + Seamless Co-op

WASD is being built around PC play with Seamless Co-op. It is designed as an external, read-only companion and does not operate during normal vanilla online play.

In development

Features

Useful information without constant wiki-checking.

Live awareness

Character stats, equipment, progression, bosses, items and other relevant game-state information while you play.

Spoiler controls

Choose between Off, Vague, Category and Full guidance depending on how much you want revealed.

Route mapping

Track where you have travelled and review your path through an area.

Missable guidance

Surface NPC steps, progression risks and things you may want to do before moving on.

Build guidance

See relevant levelling, equipment and upgrade information based on your current character.

Session analysis

Review deaths, time played, route history and other session statistics after you finish playing.

The project

Developed independently. Shaped by player feedback.

WASD is currently developed independently, with features and future game support influenced by community feedback, requests and demand.
