WASD

World Awareness & State Display

A free community-built live game companion that understands your playthrough.

First supported game: Dark Souls III

View on GitHub View roadmap

Live awareness

Track game state, progression, items, bosses and your route while you play.

Spoiler control

Choose how much guidance you want, from subtle hints to full information.

Community project

WASD is free to use, with development shaped by community feedback and support.
